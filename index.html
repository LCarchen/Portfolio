Building an AI-search visibility programme from zero

Sirelo connects people planning an international move with moving companies in 60+ countries. It had twenty years of data and real industry standing, yet AI assistants only mentioned it when the user already typed its name. Nobody in the company was measuring this.

MeasureA weekly citation test

30 buyer queries in 5 intent clusters, run every week on ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Mode, with the sentiment of every answer logged. I revised the method twice as the data came in and kept failed weeks in the log as failures.

ResearchA demand map

181 keywords across 7 markets, classified by intent and checked against Search Console. It exposed pages that ranked but didn't answer, like a UK calculator query with 41,500 impressions and 3 clicks. Those gaps set the content priorities.

CreateAnswer-first B2B pages

Pages for moving companies on lead sources, lead pricing, reviews and UK coverage. Each one opens with the answer in two or three sentences, uses questions as headings and ends with one next step, so an AI engine can lift it cleanly. I then adapted them into a LinkedIn series.

ReputationA Trustpilot programme

A KPI framework, a census of all 52 reviews sorted into 7 complaint classes, response templates and a 7-week invitation schedule. It brought in the first 5-star partner review.

CommunityReddit, done openly

I read about 1,900 posts before writing a word, then joined conversations in 4 subreddits as a disclosed brand voice. No comment was removed, and one turned into a live sales conversation.

LifecycleA 9-email onboarding flow

For moving companies earning the “Sirelo Verified” label, with branches for partners who never start or drop out halfway. I also recommended holding the launch email until partners could actually see their label.

What I took from it. The biggest lever wasn't the company's own content. It was which sources each engine chose to read. Google AI Mode leaned on Sirelo's own sites, ChatGPT leaned on review platforms, and the answers about the brand changed accordingly. That shifted the plan toward reviews and third-party presence.

Natura Global: traffic that doesn't buy

A sustainable skincare brand expanding across Europe had 21,000 campaign sessions and a 0.8% conversion rate. I mapped the data to the funnel to find where people dropped.

Scale runs to 1.2%. The problem was conversion, not traffic: TikTok had the best click-through (4.1%) and the lowest conversion of the social platforms (0.3%). I recommended faster mobile pages, guest checkout with shipping costs shown up front, abandoned-cart emails, local content for Germany, Spain and France, and moving budget away from display.
